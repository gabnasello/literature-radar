## Maleimide-Functionalized Conjugated Polymers Enable Covalent Biotic-Abiotic Integration to Boost Microbial Electrosynthesis.

PMID: 42963330 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42963330/

**Summary**

This study investigates covalent integration of maleimide-functionalized conjugated polymers with Shewanella oneidensis to enhance microbial electrosynthesis. The approach utilized in situ click reactions with cytochrome thiols to create stable bio-interfaces. Results showed doubled bacterial adhesion, 23-fold increased electron transfer, and 6-fold higher electrochemical reduction compared to unmodified bacteria. The covalent strategy significantly improved charge output and succinate yield while upregulating respiratory proteins.

**Key findings**

- Covalent maleimide-functionalized conjugated polymers doubled bacterial adhesion via in situ click reactions with cytochrome thiols
- 23-fold increase in electron transfer and 6-fold improvement in electrochemical reduction relative to bare S. oneidensis
- 2-fold enhancement in total charge output and succinate yield with upregulation of respiratory protein complexes

**Scientific significance**

This covalent biotic-abiotic interface engineering strategy establishes a molecular-level approach for optimizing microbial electrosynthesis, advancing sustainable carbon conversion through enhanced electron transfer mechanisms.

## Immune Mechanisms of Oral Tolerance in the Prevention of IgE-Mediated Food Allergy.

PMID: 43044855 | Journal: The Journal of allergy and clinical immunology
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43044855/

**Summary**

This review examines immune mechanisms underlying oral tolerance in preventing IgE-mediated food allergy. It synthesizes how early dietary antigen exposure establishes durable tolerance through coordinated cellular pathways: transcellular antigen transport via goblet cell-associated passages, tolerogenic antigen presentation by RORγt+ APCs and cDCs, regulatory T cell induction, and germinal center regulation of antibody responses. Genetic and environmental factors modulate these pathways, while infections can disrupt tolerance. The study highlights tolerance and allergy as competing immune states stabilized by redundant regulatory circuits.

**Key findings**

- Early dietary antigen exposure during infancy prevents IgE-mediated food allergy through oral tolerance mechanisms
- Oral tolerance requires coordinated activity of transcellular antigen transport, tolerogenic APCs, regulatory T cells, and germinal center regulation
- Infections can disrupt established oral tolerance by reprogramming tolerogenic antigen-presenting cells

**Scientific significance**

This review provides a comprehensive model of multilayered immune mechanisms that govern oral tolerance, clarifying how dietary antigen exposure can prevent food allergy while highlighting the critical role of redundant regulatory circuits in maintaining immune homeostasis.

## Layered Precursor-Guided Growth of Nondegenerate Amorphous In2O3 Approaching the Thickness Limit.

PMID: 42963332 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42963332/

**Summary**

This study investigates precise growth of ultrathin amorphous indium oxide (In2O3) using layered precursor-guided synthesis. By oxidizing a layered InSe precursor within BEOL-compatible thermal conditions, the researchers achieved atomically smooth In2O3 films as thin as 0.63 nm. The material exhibited nondegenerate electronic properties with low carrier density (2.8 × 1018 cm-3), minimal interface traps, and high electrostatic uniformity. These findings enable sub-3 nm channel transistors with near-zero threshold voltage and low hysteresis, advancing ultrathin oxide semiconductor applications.

**Key findings**

- Atomically smooth In2O3 films as thin as 0.63 nm achieved via layered precursor oxidation
- Nondegenerate electronic properties with carrier density of 2.8 × 1018 cm-3 and low interface trap density
- Sub-3 nm channel transistors showing near-zero threshold voltage and negligible hysteresis (<10 mV)

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes a scalable method for ultrathin amorphous oxide semiconductors with enhanced electronic properties, enabling next-generation nanoelectronic devices with improved stability and performance.

## Human replisomes direct a dedicated leading-strand primase for fork restart.

PMID: 43015477 | Journal: Nature
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43015477/

**Summary**

This study investigates how human replication forks restart after stalling, focusing on the role of the replisome. Using purified human proteins, the authors reconstituted fork stalling and restart to demonstrate that the CMG helicase directs the primase-polymerase PRIMPOL for efficient leading-strand priming during fork restart. The interaction between PRIMPOL and replication protein A was found non-essential, while direct CMG-PRIMPOL binding ensures minimal post-replicative gaps. Ablating this interaction abolished fork restart in vitro and DNA damage tolerance in cells.

**Key findings**

- CMG helicase directs PRIMPOL for efficient replication fork restart
- Direct CMG-PRIMPOL binding targets PRIMPOL specifically for leading-strand priming
- Ablating CMG-PRIMPOL interaction abolishes fork restart in vitro and PRIMPOL-dependent DNA damage tolerance

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes PRIMPOL as an integrated replisome component dedicated to leading-strand priming, providing mechanistic insight into how replication forks are restarted after stalling to maintain genomic stability.

## Dynamic Molecular Scaffold Enables Thermomechanical Stress Regulation and Adaptive Defect Healing for Stable Perovskite Solar Cells.

PMID: 42963917 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42963917/

**Summary**

This study investigates thermomechanical stress regulation in perovskite solar cells (PSCs) using a dynamic molecular scaffold. The researchers developed a crosslinked polydisulfide scaffold via in situ polymerization of DL-α-lipoic acid-N-hydroxysuccinimide ester (LA-NHS) at grain boundaries. The scaffold buffers thermal stress, anchors the perovskite lattice, and passivates defects through reversible disulfide exchange. Optimized PSCs achieved 27.19% power conversion efficiency with 91.71% retention after 150 thermal cycles (15–100°C) and 85.72% after 1500 hours at 85°C, demonstrating enhanced thermal stability and scalability.

**Key findings**

- In situ polymerization of LA-NHS establishes a crosslinked polydisulfide scaffold at grain boundaries
- The scaffold buffers thermal stress, anchors the perovskite lattice, and passivates defects via reversible disulfide exchange
- PSCs retained 91.71% efficiency after 150 thermal cycles (15–100°C) and 85.72% after 1500 hours at 85°C

**Scientific significance**

This work provides a dynamic molecular scaffold strategy for managing thermomechanical degradation in perovskite solar cells, enabling enhanced thermal stability and scalable high-efficiency photovoltaics through stress buffering, lattice anchoring, and adaptive defect healing.

## Hadean mantle depletion and 3.95-Gyr subduction implied by North China rocks.

PMID: 43015479 | Journal: Nature
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43015479/

**Summary**

This study investigates the timing and processes of early Earth crust formation by analyzing 3.95-Gyr-old North China Craton rocks. Using geochemical and isotopic data from trondhjemitic gneiss, the research identifies elevated δ18O and positive εHf(t) values, indicating a Hadean mantle depleted source. The findings suggest rapid subduction of oceanic crust at high pressure, providing evidence for early plate tectonics starting by 3.95 Gyr ago.

**Key findings**

- 3.95-Gyr-old trondhjemitic gneiss from North China Craton
- Elevated δ18O values (+6.77 ± 0.23‰) and positive εHf(t) values (+1.45 ± 0.27)
- High-pressure origin via rapid subduction of oceanic crust

**Scientific significance**

The study provides direct evidence of Hadean mantle depletion and early subduction processes, advancing understanding of Earth's crustal evolution and the onset of plate tectonics by 3.95 billion years ago.

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes a method to accurately convert WVTR measurements to intrinsic water vapor permeability by accounting for apparatus resistance, resolving a critical limitation in assessing membrane breathability for skin-interfaced electronics.
