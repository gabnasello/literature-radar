In vitro cell-based study

By tracking plasma neutralization across multiple donors over years, researchers observed initial narrow specificity (often targeting IFN-α8) that broadened in breadth and potency. **Key findings**

- Neutralizing autoantibodies against IFN-α initially target narrow specificities, often IFN-α8, but broaden over time.
- Affinity maturation produces both subtype-restricted and broadly reactive antibody classes.
- Antibodies engaging non-competing epitopes synergistically block IFN-α signaling and may trigger Fc-effector functions.

**Study type**

Longitudinal human plasma tracking with monoclonal antibody analyses and structural characterization

**Scientific significance**

The study elucidates molecular mechanisms underlying the development and functional synergy of anti-IFN-α autoantibodies, revealing how specificity broadens to enhance pathogenic potential in autoimmune contexts.
