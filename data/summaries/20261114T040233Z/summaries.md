## Emerging Lipid Nanoparticle Targeting Strategies for Extrahepatic Delivery of RNA Therapeutics.

PMID: 42894277 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42894277/

**Summary**

This review examines strategies to redirect lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) for extrahepatic RNA delivery, addressing challenges like hepatotropism and limited cell specificity. It evaluates formulation chemistry, surface engineering, and administration routes to achieve tissue-specific targeting in organs such as spleen, lungs, and tumors. The study highlights key determinants of LNP tropism and clinical translation pathways while identifying major challenges in biodistribution and safety.

**Key findings**

- Conventional LNPs exhibit intrinsic hepatotropism limiting extrahepatic RNA delivery
- Surface functionalization and lipid chemistry modifications enable tissue-specific LNP targeting
- Clinical translation faces challenges including cell-resolved biodistribution and repeated-dose immunogenicity

**Scientific significance**

This work provides a systematic framework for optimizing LNP design to achieve precise extrahep:atic RNA delivery, advancing the therapeutic potential of RNA medicines beyond hepatic applications.

PMID: 42998621 | Journal: Biomaterials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42998621/

**Summary**

This study evaluates a c-Met-targeting aptamer-drug conjugate (SL1-MMAE) for treating primary and metastatic uveal melanoma. In vitro and in vivo models demonstrated potent tumor suppression, significant survival prolongation, and robust intraocular tumor regression with minimal ocular toxicity after intravitreal injection. The ApDC's small size enabled superior tumor penetration and reduced off-target effects, showing advantages over clinical ADCs for intraocular delivery.

**Key findings**

- SL1-Musing the c-Met-targeting aptamer-drug conjugate suppressed tumor growth in subcutaneous and liver metastasis models
- A single intravitreal injection of SL1-MMAE induced tumor regression in orthotopic intraocular models without ocular toxicity
- The ApDC demonstrated superior antitumor efficacy and ocular biocompatibility compared to the clinical ADC ABBV-399

**Study type**

In vitro and in vivo models

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes c-Met-targeting aptamer-drug conjugates as a viable platform for vision-preserving therapy in uveal melanoma, leveraging targeted delivery and reduced ocular toxicity for both primary and metastatic disease.

**Study type**

In vitro and in vivo studies

By engineering heteroatom-doped carbon hosts with abundant closed pores, the material redirects expansion stress inward, suppresses electrode swelling, and improves Li+ kinetics. The nitrogen-doped composite achieved 1756.5 mAh g-1 capacity, 868.4 m. Ah g-1 at 3 C, and reduced thickness expansion from 38.4% to 13.1% after 50 cycles, demonstrating scalable performance in 6 kg batches for NCM811 batteries.

**Key findings**

- Closed nanopores in heteroatom-doped carbon hosts redirect lithiation-induced stress inward, suppressing electrode swelling
- Nitrogen-doping enhances carbon interlayer spacing and electronic conductivity, improving Li+ insertion/extraction kinetics
- The composite achieved 1756.5 mAh g-1 reversible capacity and 868.4 mAh g-1 at 3 C with 13.1% thickness expansion after 50 cycles

**Study type**

Material design and synthesis

**Scientific significance**

## Common variation of cell abundances and gene expression in the human striatum.

PMID: 43020022 | Journal: Cell
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43020022/

**Summary**

This study used single-nucleus RNA sequencing on 3.9 million nuclei from 178 adults to investigate inter-individual variation in the human striatum and prefrontal cortex. It found that astrocyte, microglia, and OPC proportions were consistently higher in brains with more of these cell types across gray matter regions, OPCs declined with age, and age could be predicted from gene expression with a median error of 6.6 years. The data also revealed cell-type-specific genetic variants influencing gene expression.

**Key findings**

- Astrocyte, microglia, and oligodendrocyte precursor cell proportions were consistently elevated across gray matter regions in brains with higher counts in one region
- Oligodendrocyte precursor cell proportions decreased by 40% between ages 30-80 in both cortex and striatum
- Donor age could be predicted from RNA expression patterns with a median absolute error of 6.6 years across cell types

**Study type**

Single-nucleus RNA sequencing

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes a comprehensive atlas of cell-type-specific genetic variation and gene expression patterns in human striatum and prefrontal cortex, providing a foundational resource for understanding brain development, aging, and disease susceptibility.
