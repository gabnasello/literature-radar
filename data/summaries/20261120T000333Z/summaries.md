- 3000-hour durability at 10 mA cm-2 in PEMWE systems

**Scientific significance**

## The deep Earth origin of the Great Unconformity.

**Summary**

This study investigates the deep Earth processes driving the Great Unconformity, a globally significant erosional surface preceding the Cambrian explosion. Using mantle convection models, the authors demonstrate that long-wavelength dynamic topography caused continental-scale uplift during the Cryogenian and subsequent subsidence in the Phanerozoic. These tectonic motions explain the Great Unconformity's formation and preservation, linking deep Earth dynamics to surface geological features.

**Key findings**

- Mantle convection-driven dynamic topography caused Cryogenian continental uplift
- Predicted prolonged Neoproterozoic uplift and Phanerozoic subsidence across Laurentia, Baltica, and North China
- The vertical motion scenario explains the Great Unconformity's preservation and formation

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes a direct link between mantle convection and the formation of the Great Unconformity, providing a deep Earth mechanism for a major surface geological feature.
