## Sub-nm2 Ferroelectric Domains via Charged 180° Walls in ZrO2.

PMID: 42908261 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42908261/

**Summary**

This study investigates the lateral and vertical compression of ferroelectric domains in ZrO2 thin films. Using direct observation, researchers identified charged 180° walls (HH and TT) that create sub-nm2 domain footprints. The HH walls exhibit electrostatic stabilization via interstitial oxygen, enabling ultralow domain wall mobility (1.6 meV) compared to TT walls (2.23 meV), suggesting potential for low-voltage nanoelectronics applications.

**Key findings**

- Charged 180° walls in ZrO2 thin films achieve sub-nm2 domain footprints
- HH walls are electrostatically stabilized by interstitial oxygen atoms
- HH walls exhibit significantly lower mobility (1.6 meV) than TT walls (22.3 meV)

**Scientific significance**
