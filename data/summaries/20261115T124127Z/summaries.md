## Cell-state-specific metabolic networks govern ferroptosis versus apoptosis in small cell lung cancer.

PMID: 42914976 | Journal: Cell
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42914976/

**Summary**

This study investigated metabolic heterogeneity in small cell lung cancer (SCLC) cell states. Using transcriptomic and metabolomic analyses, it identified universal cysteine dependency across neuroendocrine (NE) and non-NE SCLC states. NE cells with low ASCL1 levels underwent ferroptosis upon cysteine depletion, while ASCL1high cells underwent apoptosis and resisted ferroptosis due to GCH1-mediated BH4/BH2 upregulation. Targeting cysteine and BH4/BH2 synthesis reduced tumor growth in xenografts.

**Key findings**

- SCLC cells exhibit universal cysteine dependency across neuroendocrine and non-neuroendocrine states
- ASCL1low NE cells undergo ferroptosis upon cysteine depletion, whereas ASCL1high cells undergo apoptosis and resist ferroptosis
- GCH1 transcriptional activation by ASCL1 increases BH4/BH2, conferring ferroptosis resistance

**Study type**

Integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic analyses

**Scientific significance**

The findings establish metabolic state as a determinant of cell death susceptibility in SCLC, revealing a mechanism for targeted therapeutic intervention through cysteine and antioxidant pathways.
