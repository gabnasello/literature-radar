## Ultralow Loading Iridium Doped Hollandite Manganese Oxide Nanowires for Efficient Acidic Oxygen Evolution Reaction.

PMID: 43004155 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43004155/

**Summary**

This study investigates ultralow-loading iridium-doped hollandite manganese oxide nanowires as efficient acidic oxygen evolution reaction (OER) catalysts. The researchers integrated 3 at% iridium into a manganese dioxide framework, reducing the overpotential at 10 mA cm-2 from 580 mV to 303 mV compared to undoped α-MnO2. The catalyst demonstrates significantly higher iridium mass activity than commercial IrOx. Dissolution measurements and spectroscopic analyses revealed modest material loss (79% Ir, 76% Mn retained after 6 h) with iridium remaining oxidized and structurally stable, while manganese undergoes pH- and potential-dependent reduction and structural disorder.

**Key findings**

- Ultralow iridium loading (3 at%, <16 µgIr cm-2) in hollandite MnO2 framework reduces OER overpotential from 580 mV to 303 mV at 10 mA cm-2
- The catalyst achieves iridium mass activity one order of magnitude higher than commercial IrOx
- Modest dissolution (79% Ir, 76% Mn retained after 6 h) with iridium remaining oxidized and structurally stable despite pH- and potential-dependent manganese reduction

**Scientific significance**

This work demonstrates that ultralow iridium loading within a manganese oxide framework can significantly enhance acidic OER catalysis while maintaining structural stability, providing a pathway to more sustainable and efficient catalysts for hydrogen production.
