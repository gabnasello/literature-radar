## Long-term local circulation of an Andes virus lineage in northern Patagonia is linked to the M/V Hondius outbreak.

PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019392/

**Summary**

This study investigated the long-term circulation of an Andes virus (ANDV) lineage in northern Patagonia, linking it to a 2026 cruise ship hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS) outbreak. Retrospective analysis showed person-to-person transmission in a 2018 Argentina outbreak and a common ANDV lineage spanning cases from 2018 to 2025. The cruise ship outbreak's source remains unresolved, emphasizing the need for enhanced viral surveillance in ANDV-endemic regions.

**Key findings**

- A common ANDV lineage linked 2018 Argentina and 2026 cruise ship HPS outbreaks
- Person-to-person transmission occurred in the 2018 Argentina outbreak
- ANDV circulation in northern Patagonia spanned 2018 to 2025 without widespread community transmission

**Scientific significance**

This study demonstrates persistent ANDV circulation in northern Patagonia over seven years, revealing prolonged transmission dynamics that challenge assumptions about hantavirus outbreak containment and highlighting the importance of regional surveillance for early detection.

## Nonfullerene Acceptor-Iridium Coordination Polymer as a Triplet Sensitizer for Efficient Organic Photovoltaics.

PMID: 42947636 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42947636/

**Summary**

This study investigates triplet exciton utilization in organic solar cells (OSCs) using a nonfullerene acceptor-iridium coordination polymer (BTP-N-Ir) as a triplet sensitizer. The approach involves ternary OSCs where the polymer facilitates triplet exciton generation via a small singlet-triplet energy gap, transferring to an intra-moiety excited state to enhance photocurrents. The system achieves 20.37% efficiency, representing a significant advancement in triplet-sensitized OSCs.

**Key findings**

- BTP-N-Ir demonstrates a small singlet-triplet energy gap enabling long-lived triplet exciton generation
- Triplet excitons transfer to an intra-musing excited state of the acceptor, dissociating into free charges
- The triplet-sensitized OSCs reach 20.37% efficiency, the highest reported for triplet-based systems

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes a novel triplet-exciton transfer pathway through intra-moiety excited states, enhancing triplet utilization efficiency in organic photovoltaics without requiring additional charge separation mechanisms.
