**Summary**

This study demonstrates a method to dynamically control liquid wettability and droplet mobility on solid surfaces using crystalline packing in semicrystalline polymers. By manipulating the melting and cooling pathways of poly(octadecyl acrylate) (PODAc) within a porous polymer coating, the researchers achieve reversible transitions between hydrophilic (contact angle θW ∼ 85°), hydrophobic (θW ∼ 104°), and amphiphobic slippery states. Integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic approaches revealed a universal dependency on exogenous cysteine/cystine across SCLC cell states.
