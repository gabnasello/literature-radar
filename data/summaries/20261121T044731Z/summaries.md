## Twist-angle-dependent quantum phase diagrams in moiré bilayer MoTe2.

PMID: 42983090 | Journal: Nature
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42983090/

**Summary**

This study investigates how the twist angle in twisted bilayer MoTe2 influences quantum phase transitions. Using transport measurements across 3.79°-5.76° twist angles, researchers observed an evolution from valley-polarized fractional topological states to valley-degenerate correlated states and superconductivity. The phase diagram reveals transitions between fractional quantum anomalous Hall states, integer quantum anomalous Hall insulators, and superconductivity as twist angle increases.

**Key findings**

- Evolution from valley-polarized fractional topological states to valley-degenerate correlated states and superconductivity across 3.79°-5.76° twist angles
- At small twist angles, partially filled Chern bands host fractional quantum anomalous Hall states following the Jain sequence
- Superconductivity emerges near νh = 1 at twist angles above ~5.6°, resembling twisted bilayer WSe2 phase diagrams

**Scientific significance**

This work establishes a unified mechanism for twist-angle-driven quantum phase evolution in moiré systems, linking fractional topology, symmetry breaking, and superconductivity through systematic phase diagram mapping.
