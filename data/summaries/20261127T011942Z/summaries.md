## Ultralow Loading Iridium Doped Hollandite Manganese Oxide Nanowires for Efficient Acidic Oxygen Evolution Reaction.

PMID: 43004155 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43004155/

{
  "summary": "This study investigates ultralow-loading iridium-doped hollandite manganese oxide nanowires for acidic oxygen evolution reaction (OER) catalysis. The researchers developed a catalyst with 3 atomic percent iridium (<16 µg Ir cm-2) integrated into a manganese dioxide framework. It reduced the overpotential at 10 mA cm-2 from 580 mV to 303 mV compared to undoped α-MnO2 and achieved Ir mass activity 10 times higher than commercial IrOx. The catalyst showed modest dissolution (79% Ir, 76% Mn retained after 6 h), with iridium remaining oxidized and bound to the framework while manganese underwent pH- and potential-dependent reduction and structural disorder. The findings emphasize the need to evaluate dissolution, reconstruction, and oxidation state together for designing efficient iridium-free catalysts.",

    "Reduced OER overpotential to 303 mV at 10 mA cm-2 from 580 mV in undoped α-MnO2",
    "Modest dissolution (79% Ir, 76% Mn retained after 6 h) with iridium remaining oxidized and bound to the framework"
  ],
  "scientific_significance": "This work demonstrates that ultralow iridium loading in manganese oxide frameworks can achieve high OER activity while maintaining structural stability, providing critical insights for developing durable, iridium-efficient catalysts for acidic electrolyzers."

## Diet at disease onset determines tissue inflammatory memory.

PMID: 42957863 | Journal: Nature
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42957863/

{
  "summary": "This study investigates whether brief dietary changes during acute inflammation influence long-term tissue immune memory. Using lean mice with type 17 skin inflammation, researchers found that a transient high-fat diet (HFD) exposure within a 4-day window at disease onset exacerbates inflammation to levels seen in chronic obesity. HFD outside this window or switching obese mice to low-fat diet during the window reduces disease severity. Mechanistically, HFD recruits neutrophils that express Nlrp3 and Il1b, activating a neutrophil-NLRP3-IL-1β axis that licenses pathogenic TH17 cells. After recovery, chromatin-poised tissue-resident memory T cells persist, causing exaggerated inflammation upon recurrence despite subsequent low-fat diet. The findings demonstrate that short-lived dietary states during specific temporal windows can durably reprogram immune responses.",
  "key_findings": [
    "Transient high-fat diet exposure within a 4-day window at disease onset exacerbates acute skin inflammation to chronic obesity levels",
    "HFD outside this window or switching obese mice to low-fat diet during the window reduces disease severity",
    "Neutrophil-NLRP3-IL-1β axis licenses pathogenic TH17 cells, with chromatin-poised tissue-resident memory T cells persisting post-recovery"
  ],
  "scientific_significance": "This work establishes that transient dietary states during narrow temporal windows can durably reprogram immune responses, revealing a mechanism by which short-lived physiological changes become embedded in tissue immune memory."
}
