## Emergence of Chern metal in a moiré Kondo lattice.

PMID: 42956076 | Journal: Science
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42956076/

**Summary**

This study investigates the emergence of a Chern metal in frustrated moiré Kondo lattices using angle-aligned MoTe2/WSe2 bilayers. It identifies a compressible state with nearly quantized Hall resistance and finite longitudinal resistance, indicating a bad metallic bulk. Nonlocal transport and magnetic circular dichroism confirm chiral edge states and a magnetization plateau before Kondo breakdown.

**Key findings**

- Observation of a compressible Chern metal at the onset of magnetic Kondo breakdown in MoTe2/WSe2 bilayers
- Nearly quantized Hall resistance and finite longitudinal resistance indicating a bad metallic bulk
- Magnetization plateau preceding Kondo breakdown, confirmed by magnetic circular dichroism

**Scientific significance**

The study demonstrates how moiré engineering can stabilize Chern metals through the interplay of band topology and K. interactions, providing a pathway for designing exotic quantum phases.

## Amorphization Unlocks Active Sites in Organic Small Molecules for Ah-Level Aqueous Ammonium-Ion Batteries.

PMID: 42920103 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42920103/

**Summary**

This study investigates amorphization of organic small molecules to enhance active site exposure in aqueous ammonium-ion batteries. By disrupting π-π stacking in tetraamino-dihydrophenazine (TDT), the approach improved electrolyte accessibility and activated buried redox sites. The amorphous TDT achieved high capacity retention (87.1% after 817 cycles) and demonstrated practical Ah-level performance in pouch cells, indicating durable, high-capacity aqueous ammonium-ion batteries.

**Key findings**

- Amorphous TDT achieved 242.8 mAh g-1 capacity at 0.1 A g-1
- Retained 87.1% capacity after 817 cycles (2900 h) with high surface area (738 m2 g-1)
- TDT||CuFe-PBA full cell maintained 100% capacity over 100,000 cycles at 5 A g-1

**Scientific significance**

Amorphization of organic small molecules enables high-capacity, long-cycle aqueous ammonium-ion batteries by activating buried redox sites through disrupted molecular stacking.

**Summary**

This study investigates whether MnF2 exhibits altermagnetism using polarized inelastic neutron scattering (INS). The research identifies a magnon band splitting due to long-range dipolar coupling and a nonzero chirality in the neutron scattering cross section that reverses sign between magnon modes. These findings confirm MnF.2 as an altermagnet, demonstrating polarized INS's utility for characterizing altermagnets through magnon spectral fine structure.

**Key findings**

- MnF2 shows magnon band splitting from long-range dipolar coupling
- Neutron scattering cross section exhibits nonzero chirality reversing sign between magnon modes
- Polarized INS confirms MnF2 as an altermagnet through magnon spectral fine structure

**Scientific significance**

This work provides direct spectroscopic evidence for altermagnetism in MnF2, highlighting polarized neutron scattering's capability to resolve magnon band splitting and chirality signatures essential for identifying altermagnets.

## Quantification of Magnetic Interactions in van der Waals Heterostructures Using Lorentz Transmission Electron Microscopy and Off-Axis Electron Holography.

PMID: 42920391 | Journal: Advanced Materials
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42920391/

**Summary**

This study quantifies magnetic interactions in Fe3GeTe2/graphite/Fe3GeTe2 heterostructures using cross-sectional Lorentz transmission electron microscopy and off-axis electron holography. It measures local projected magnetic induction within layers, revealing domain alignment weakens with increasing separation, yielding a stray-field coupling length scale of 37±7 nm. This corresponds to a 23% reduction in interlayer magnetic induction relative to bulk FGT and a near-surface reduction up to ∼100 nm.

**Key findings**

- Domain alignment weakens with increasing FGT separation, yielding a stray-field coupling length scale of : 37±7 nm
- This length scale corresponds to a 23% reduction in interlayer magnetic induction relative to bulk FGT
- Magnetic induction reduces relative to bulk FGT up to ∼100 nm from a surface

**Scientific significance**

The study provides quantitative characterization of magnetic coupling in stacked van der Waals magnets, establishing a critical length scale for interlayer interactions that informs device design.
