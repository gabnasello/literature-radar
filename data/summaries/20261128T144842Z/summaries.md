## Diet at disease onset determines tissue inflammatory memory.

PMID: 42957863 | Journal: Nature
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42957863/

  "summary": "This study investigates whether brief dietary changes during acute inflammation influence long-term tissue immune memory. Using lean mice with type 17 skin inflammation, researchers found that a transient high-fat diet (HFD) exposure within a 4-day window at disease onset exacerbates inflammation to levels comparable with chronic obesity.
